## 2. When paid starts and how it ramps
- Bootstrapped SaaS: small tests can start at $0 MRR. Serious testing usually starts at **$5–10k MRR**, and scaling at **$10–20k+ MRR** once retention and payback are proven. [A] stealwhatworks.com, 2025
- Usual first channel is **Google Search** (high intent, low minimum spend). Guidance: about 40% of paid budget below $1M ARR, falling to about 25% at $1–5M ARR as LinkedIn and review sites are added. [A] growthspree, 2026
- **LinkedIn** usually comes 2nd once the ICP is clear, typically around $1M ARR. Video and retargeting come later. [E]
- Ramp: paid spend tends to grow 1–1.5× as fast as revenue in the early years, then levels off at about 4–8% of ARR. [E]

## 3. Channel mix (share of the B2B paid budget)
- LinkedIn's share of B2B ad budgets grew from **31% (H1 2024) to 39% (end of 2024)**. Non-branded Google Search averaged **37%**. [S] Dreamdata LinkedIn Ads Benchmarks 2025, dreamdata.io/blog/dreamdata-2025-linkedin-ads-benchmarks-report-highlights
- LinkedIn is **41% of B2B paid-social budgets** (2026, up from 39%). [S] Dreamdata 2026 report, via ppcblogpro.com
- ROAS: LinkedIn 113%, Google Search 78%, Meta 29%. Cost per company influenced is 25% higher on Google and 70% higher on Meta than on LinkedIn. [S] Dreamdata 2025
- Typical niche B2B SaaS mix: Search 35–45%, LinkedIn 30–40%, review sites 5–10%, YouTube 5–10%, Reddit 0–8%, Meta 0–5% (mostly retargeting), programmatic 0–5%. [E, anchored on Dreamdata]

## 5. Cadence and seasonality
- B2B LinkedIn spend share by quarter: **Q1 27.5%, Q2 18%, Q4 31%**. Q2 is the most cost-efficient quarter. Q3 has the best pipeline ROI (6.0×, versus about 2.5× in other quarters), with a September peak. Q4 has the lowest efficiency. [S] HockeyStack 2025
- Pattern: **always-on** search and retargeting, plus **4–8 week bursts** for launches and events. Typical quarterly LinkedIn campaign length is 4–12 weeks. [E]
- Europe and the Nordics slow down sharply in **July** (Finnish holiday month) and the **last two weeks of December**, so plan spend 30–50% below normal. [E]

## 6. Lags, buying cycles, adstock
- Self-serve/PLG: most trial conversions happen around **day 7**. A 14-day trial peaks on **days 8–10**, and conversions are about 1% after day 14. Median trial→paid is 18.5%. Opt-in trials convert at 8.9% and card-required trials at 31.4%. [A] 1capture.io 2025; ChartMogul SaaS Conversion Report (chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report)
- Sales-led: the average B2B journey is **211 days**, and about 314 days for large companies. From LinkedIn: first impression → revenue 320 days, ad engagement → revenue 235 days, first conversion → revenue 219 days. [S] Dreamdata 2025. The 2026 report says the average journey is now 272 days. [A]
- Sales cycle by ACV: $50–100k takes about **90–150 days**, median about 120. Over $100k takes 6–9+ months, and procurement adds 30–45 days. [A] humanr.ai / growthspree 2025–26
- MMM adstock: industry practice uses half-lives of **2–5 weeks**, while academic studies find **7–12 weeks**. Search decays within about 1 week; awareness channels last several weeks. Decay 0.5 ≈ 1-week half-life, 0.7 ≈ 2 weeks, 0.8 ≈ 3 weeks, 0.9 ≈ 7 weeks. [A] ppc.land/adstock; Wikipedia "Advertising adstock"
- Google Meridian: default max_lag is 2–10 periods. For B2B long cycles, raise it to **12+ weeks** and/or use binomial decay. [S] developers.google.com/meridian/docs/advanced-modeling/set-max-lag-parameter; github.com/google/meridian discussions

[S] blog.hamina.com/wlpc-wi-fi-awards
- **Cisco Live EMEA** (Amsterdam, Feb 9–14 2025) and **Cisco Live US** (Las Vegas, Jun 8–12 2025). [S] newsroom.cisco.com, ciscolive.com. **Mobility Field Day 13** was May 7–9 2025 (presenters: Arista, Cisco, Juniper, Ubiquiti and others). [S] techfieldday.com/mfd
